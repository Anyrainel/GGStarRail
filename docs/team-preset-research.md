# Team preset research

Reviewed on 2026-10-12 against the local released character catalog. The single subscribable preset lives in `src/config/presets/teams/flagship.json`; all IDs are source IDs, with canonical odd Trailblazer IDs. It contains all 53 teams under the stable preset ID `flagship-teams`.

## Selection policy

One premium lineup per offensive core. The first slot is the chosen core; the remaining slots roughly follow secondary damage, amplification, sustain. No ownership-based or low-cost substitutions. These are curated recommendations from authored character synergy discussions, not a mathematical claim of universal optimum. Encounter, speed tuning, Eidolons, Light Cones and manual rotations can change the ranking. Rows contain characters only and do not silently prescribe Eidolons or signatures.

A character can be both a main damage dealer and an amplifier. Accordingly, the same composition may be listed under multiple cores (for example Castorice/Evernight and the DoT trio). Sustainless variants are deliberately omitted from this initial general-purpose collection.

## Evidence and coverage

The source index below preserves direct guide links for every lineup. Prydwen is used as the authored analysis source, rather than its crowd-sampled usage tables or unrelated SEO summaries. Most older pages display a 31 May 2026 profile stamp while their synergy sections include newer characters; this is a profile maintenance stamp, not independent proof of a full current-patch recalculation. Newer partner guides are checked where an old page lacks them. Each lineup is a curator's assembly of the guide's strongest compatible synergies, not necessarily a literal screenshot of a guide team card.

The collection covers 53 offensive cores, including four-star carries and Yanqing. A four-star core is eligible on exactly the same terms as a five-star core: the lineup uses premium partners rather than budget substitutes. Pure supports are not forced into carry entries. Robin Summeretto and Welt are treated as supports; their specialist damage builds are outside this main-carry collection.

Four-star selections use the core guide's favored archetype: Qingque gets Sparkle and Silver Wolf with the newer ATK-buffing shield sustain; Xueyi and Sushang get Fugue/Ruan Mei/Lingsha; Arlan gets two action buffers and strong shielding; Misha gets Sunday/Sparkle with the ATK-buffing shield sustain. Yanqing keeps Aventurine to protect his damage state, with Sunday and Robin. Herta uses Jade/Robin/Lingsha for Pure Fiction; Himeko uses her Fugue/Ruan Mei Break route. Hunt March uses The Dahlia as her Nihility Shifu in a premium Break team. These rows are curated combinations of published synergy recommendations; modern generalist partner choices are inferences where the older core guide has not published new comparative calculations.

Rin's carry row uses Cerydra/Sparkle/Dan Heng Permansor Terrae and assumes intentional vertical investment, as her guide explicitly cautions against solo hypercarry Rin without Eidolons. Her standard Archer partner lineup remains separately available with Archer first. Sampo and Guinaifen keep Kafka and Hysilens as their premium DoT partners; Moze and Topaz keep Feixiao as their frequent-attacking partner rather than replacing that partner with a low-cost alternative.

Boothill's selected Break route is based on The Dahlia and Fugue's offensive synergy, with Lingsha as the Break sustain. An action-advance variant may perform better in particular single-target encounters; do not label the saved row an absolute ranking.

## Maintenance

Refresh recommendations when a relevant offensive partner, sustain, enhancement patch, or endgame ruleset changes. Keep stable preset IDs and character IDs; change the four-character composition only after reviewing the existing row's sources and the new partner's guide. Never replace a premium partner with a budget fallback merely because it is more common in sampled accounts.
