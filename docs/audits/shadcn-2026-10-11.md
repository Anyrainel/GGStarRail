## Decisions

All 7 official components differed before upgrade. The local implementations omitted upstream APIs and changed styles and markup.

User-approved fixes moved above the stock bases: accordion focus padding to shared/FocusSafeAccordionContent; localized sheet close control to shared/LocalizedSheetContent; submenu pointer-focus handling to shared/StableDropdownSubTrigger. The sheet wrapper hides the built-in English close control and provides a localized close button, preserving refs.

Removed without preservation: custom button/badge/card styling, alternate card markup/spacing, custom menu spacing/radii/colors and manual check helper, custom popover sizing/offsets, sheet sizing/overlay styles. Menu radio items now use upstream indicators. Existing custom lightweight-select and responsive-dialog remain in place.
