## roadmap

## 2026-10-21

### features
- GOODCapture / GOODScanner are now [GGScanner / GGScannerOCR](/download-scanner), respectively, with a fully redesigned interface, added Honkai: Star Rail support, and fixes for recent data and logic issues.
- Added team damage simulation and Relic optimization, with Light Cone, Relic set, and Eidolon comparisons, plus a battle timeline showing actions, Energy, and Skill Points.
- Added damage calculations and combat effects for many more Characters, Light Cones, and Relic sets. Bugs may remain, and your feedback is welcome.
- Added build and team preset subscriptions that preserve your personal changes when presets update.
- Added sign-in, feedback, and character Archive comments, with feedback visibility controls and upvotes.

### fixes
- Fixed pages that could not scroll to the bottom and back navigation in the Currency War Archive, and made large collections smoother to browse.

## 2026-10-10

### features
- Import your Characters, Light Cones, Relics, and achievement progress from GGScanner.
- Configure Cavern Relics and Planar Ornaments separately, then filter your inventory by your build preferences.
- Find Relics to upgrade or reroll, review what to keep, and send lock and discard choices to GGScanner.
- Create priority lists for Characters, Light Cones, and Relic sets.
- Browse Characters, Light Cones, Relic sets, achievements, and Currency War data.
